learning the project and checking supporting records without reading a wall of cards.

# Artifact Quality Harness Plan

- Outcome: apply one global maximum of five deterministic artifact-quality
  judging passes to every white-paper generation; preserve its result and
  cross-report trend in the immutable report manifest.
- Boundary: inspect the prepared HTML/PDF bytes and source-bound presentation
  only. Exclude model calls, content rewriting, canonical knowledge edits,
  external services, and automatic publication decisions.
- Decision: use a deterministic structural judge rather than a probabilistic
  self-critique. The output is source-bound and retrying an identical
  deterministic composition cannot legitimately improve prose. A failed first
  check may be confirmed once, but an unchanged or lower score stops early;
  the global cap remains available for future distinct candidates.
- Planned proof: manifest declares the shared cap, every generated artifact
  records 1–5 attempts, expected PDF/source criteria, pass state, and baseline,
  stable, improved, or degraded trend; repeated fixture generation records a
  stable trend and the dashboard boundary suite remains green.
